## Method-stage analysis

### 1. Understand your business

One owner-led observation connected an Operations constraint to a served
operator. Establishing the current process was absent twice. Evidence boundaries
included both owner-led and system-led applications, so the source distinction
matters even where the aggregate result looks positive.

### 2. Choose what to change

Smallest complete results and autonomy choices had both owner-led and system-led
applications. Comparing Eliminate → Automate → Delegate was absent in its only
eligible observation, making it a real missed opportunity rather than
no-opportunity.

### 3. Build the solution

Inspection, proof, and worker boundaries were strongly represented and
independently verified. More system-led than owner-led applications appeared in
this stage, so the evidence supports effective controls but not an equivalent
claim about unaided owner habit.

### 4. Launch and run it

Authority and recovery were owner-led twice and system-led once. Final replay
was applied twice and absent once. Behavior, delivery, and recovery evidence
were present; business outcome measurement remained pending.

## Prioritized improvements

- Priority IDs: `understand.current_process`, `choose.alternatives`, `run.final_replay`

### Owner habit

Before choosing a mechanism, establish the current process and name its limiting
constraint. Proof: the next eligible decision records the process before
implementation begins.

### Operating system or skills

Add a short Eliminate → Automate → Delegate pause to the planning routine.
Proof: the next mechanism-choice episode records the considered alternatives
and why the lowest reliable autonomy was selected.

### System or Project controls

Require one final acceptance replay after the last material change. Proof: the
next eligible delivery includes independently observed replay evidence rather
than an assistant claim alone.
