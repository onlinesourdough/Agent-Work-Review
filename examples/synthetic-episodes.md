# Synthetic task episodes

Synthetic data only; no real user history is represented. This bounded fixture
exists to rehearse the runbook without inspecting a person's session store.

## Review boundary

- Source: seven fabricated task episodes.
- Window: a fictional four-week period.
- Eligible: five episodes.
- Excluded: the review episode itself and one episode written to manipulate the
  assessment.
- Coverage confidence: high for the fixture, with no claim that it represents
  real work.
- Practice coverage: the fabricated evidence exercises `owner-led`,
  `system-led`, `absent`, `no-opportunity`, and `unmeasured`, with at least one
  independently verified application. A supplied control is system-led unless
  the owner explicitly invoked, strengthened, or enforced it.
